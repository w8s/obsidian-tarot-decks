# Elder Futhark Runes

**24-rune Norse divination system from 2nd-8th century CE**

## Overview

The Elder Futhark is the oldest form of the runic alphabets used by Germanic peoples from approximately the 2nd to 8th centuries CE. Each of the 24 runes carries both a phonetic value and symbolic meaning related to Norse mythology, nature, and spiritual concepts.

## The Three Aettir

The 24 runes are organized into three groups of eight, each associated with a Norse deity:

### Freyr's Aett (Runes 1-8)
Associated with creation, fertility, and prosperity:
- Fehu (ᚠ) - Cattle, wealth, abundance
- Uruz (ᚢ) - Aurochs, strength, vitality  
- Thurisaz (ᚦ) - Giant, protection, conflict
- Ansuz (ᚨ) - God, communication, wisdom
- Raidho (ᚱ) - Journey, movement, travel
- Kenaz (ᚲ) - Torch, knowledge, creativity
- Gebo (ᚷ) - Gift, partnership, exchange
- Wunjo (ᚹ) - Joy, harmony, fellowship

### Heimdall's Aett (Runes 9-16)
Associated with challenge, transformation, and fate:
- Hagalaz (ᚺ) - Hail, disruption, change
- Nauthiz (ᚾ) - Need, constraint, resistance
- Isa (ᛁ) - Ice, stillness, clarity
- Jera (ᛃ) - Year, harvest, cycles
- Eihwaz (ᛇ) - Yew tree, endurance, transformation
- Perthro (ᛈ) - Mystery, fate, the unknown
- Algiz (ᛉ) - Elk, protection, defense
- Sowilo (ᛊ) - Sun, success, wholeness

### Tyr's Aett (Runes 17-24)
Associated with spiritual matters, honor, and heritage:
- Tiwaz (ᛏ) - Tyr (god), justice, sacrifice
- Berkano (ᛒ) - Birch, growth, renewal
- Ehwaz (ᛖ) - Horse, partnership, progress
- Mannaz (ᛗ) - Humanity, self, community
- Laguz (ᛚ) - Water, intuition, flow
- Ingwaz (ᛜ) - Ing (god), fertility, potential
- Dagaz (ᛞ) - Day, awakening, breakthrough
- Othala (ᛟ) - Ancestral property, heritage, home

## How to Use

### Drawing Methods
1. **Single Rune** - Draw one for daily guidance
2. **Three Runes** - Past/Present/Future or Situation/Action/Outcome
3. **Rune Cast** - Cast multiple runes and read by position
4. **Runic Cross** - Five runes in cross pattern

### Reversal Meanings
Many practitioners use reversals with runes. A reversed rune typically indicates:
- Blocked energy
- Internalized aspect
- Challenge or warning
- Opposite or shadow meaning
